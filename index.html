What accounting services do you offer?

Certified accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, sales tax and financial consulting, tailored to individuals and businesses.

Can you help with IRS tax preparation?

Yes. We specialize in accurate and reliable tax preparation for individuals, businesses and nonprofits, in line with IRS requirements.

What should I bring to my tax appointment?

A photo ID, Social Security numbers or ITINs for everyone on the return, your W-2 and 1099 forms, last year's return, and records of any deductible expenses. If you're not sure about a document, bring it or call us first.

What notary services are available?

Professional notary services including document authentication and certification, power of attorney and apostille.

What do I need to get a document notarized?

Bring the document unsigned and a valid government-issued photo ID. Everyone who signs needs to be present in person.

What is an apostille?

An apostille certifies a public or notarized document so it is accepted in another country that is part of the Hague Apostille Convention. We help you prepare documents for apostille.

Can you help me start a business?

Yes. We handle new business filing for LLCs, corporations and nonprofits, obtain your EIN, and help you stay compliant afterwards.
